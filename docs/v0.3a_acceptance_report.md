## 4. Historical integrity findings

* **Yield availability leak found and fixed.**
  - The first rule ("next Federal Reserve business day") made observations available **before**
    ALFRED's real first vintage in 10 of 38 calibration cases per series. The causes were
    Saturday holidays observed on Friday, executive-order closures, and holiday rows published
    later.
  - It was replaced by the H.15 publication-day rule. On real recorded vintages around nine
    holiday windows, the new rule matches exactly in 35 of 38 cases, is one day late in 3, and is
    **early in 0**.
  - The calibration is a permanent replay test.
* **Provisional bars.** The 2026-10-30 bars, retrieved before D+1 00:00 New York, are stored
  PROVISIONAL. They are excluded from point-in-time queries and calculations, and a later
  retrieval produces the FINAL version.
* **Corrections.** A changed observation becomes a new version, visible only from its retrieval
  time. Earlier cutoffs still see the original.
* **Corporate actions.** These are known from ex-date 00:00 New York. Before the NVDA 10:1 ex-date
  (2024-06-10), point-in-time split-adjusted output equals raw output. Dividends with a future
  ex-date are not applied.
* **Research mode** is labelled `retrospectively_adjusted` and does not accept `as_of`.
* **Data-quality findings on the fixtures.** These are exactly the anomalies embedded in the
  synthetic data; none are unexpected.

| IWM | MISSING_SESSION | 2025-03-14 | not filled; returns into and out of the gap are null |
| XLE | EXTREME_RETURN | 2025-08-14 | +30.66%, kept and flagged |

## 7. Provider limitations

* **Alpaca (primary): live behaviour unverified.** Still unverified:
  - SIP vs IEX history on the free plan;
  - history depth;
  - the daily-bar timestamp convention;
  - delisted coverage;
  - correction behaviour.
* Alpaca corporate actions carry **no announcement date**. Actions are treated as known only
  from the ex-date, so no information is used early, but an announced future split cannot be
  anticipated.
* Massive/Polygon is the natural second adapter (paid tiers needed). Stooq is gated by a browser
  challenge, which was not circumvented. Nasdaq Data Link is paid. yfinance has personal-use
  terms and was not implemented.
* The backfill assumes the first-retrieved version equals what was originally published.
* Halts cannot be distinguished from missing data without a halt source.
* Concurrency is single-process SQLite; PostgreSQL is untested.

## 9. Release review (2026-11-02)

**Live Alpaca validation (Task 1): not performed.**
- No Alpaca credentials are configured: not in the shell, the user/machine environment, or a
  `.env` file.
- `GET data.alpaca.markets/v2/stocks/bars` without keys still returns HTTP 401.
- `test_live_v03a_alpaca_market_data` skips.
- Therefore **none** of the following has been verified, and nothing here should be read as
  Alpaca behaviour:
  - IEX vs SIP feeds, and SIP access without a paid subscription;
  - coverage depth and daily-bar timestamps;
  - pagination and the adjustment parameters;
  - the 2024-06-10 NVDA 10:1 split and dividend adjustment against live data;
  - IEX vs SIP volume.
- IEX-only data must not be treated as consolidated U.S. market data. The default feed is `sip`,
  and the first live run must record which feed actually returned data.

**Defect found and fixed (medium severity): a correction that reverts to an earlier value was
discarded.**
- *Symptom:* `MarketStore` treated an observation as unchanged if it matched **any** earlier
  version. A provider correcting A -> B and later reverting to A was dropped, so current and
  post-revert point-in-time views kept serving B.
- *Cause in the schema:* migration 0004's unique constraints on `(key, content_hash)` made the
  revert impossible to store.
- *Scope:* earlier cutoffs were never affected (no look-ahead). The error was a stale view after
  the revert.
- *Fix (`5e90883`):*
  - Duplicates are compared with the **latest** version.
  - A replay of an older version, from a retrieval (bars, corporate actions) or ALFRED vintage
    (yields) no newer than the latest, is still ignored.
  - Migration `0005_market_version_identity` makes versions unique by `(key, version)`.
- *Four regression tests:* bar revert, stale bar replay, corporate-action revert, and yield
  revision revert vs an older-vintage replay.
